Everything a Driver Needs — Hands-Free

⏱

HOS Compliance

Real-time 11-hour, 14-hour, and 30-minute break tracking. Co-Driver warns you before you approach a limit, never after.

🗺

IFTA Mileage

Automatic state-line detection logs miles per state as you drive. Export a CSV for your quarterly filing in seconds.

📸

Receipt Scanner

Point your camera at a fuel receipt. Gallons, price, and state are read automatically and saved to your fuel log.

💬

Dispatch Messaging

Say "Tell dispatch I'll be 30 minutes late" — Co-Driver sends it and logs it. Eyes stay on the road.

📋

Voice Pre-Trip Inspection

Walk through every DVIR checkpoint by voice. Co-Driver guides you step by step and logs the completed inspection.

💰

Load Profit Calculator

Ask "Is this load worth it?" with the gross rate and miles. Co-Driver calculates true profit using your actual fuel records and costs.

❤️

Fatigue Monitoring

Optional HealthKit integration reads your heart rate and sleep data to calculate a fatigue score. Your health data never leaves your device.

🔧

Maintenance Tracking

Schedule oil changes, tire rotations, and brake checks. Report an issue by voice — it's logged immediately.
