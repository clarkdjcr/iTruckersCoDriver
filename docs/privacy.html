🛡️ The Short Version

We built iTrucker's Co-Driver for truck drivers, not advertisers. We don't sell your data. We don't run analytics on your conversations. We don't have a server that stores your voice, your location, or your health information. Most of your data lives on your device and, optionally, in your own iCloud account.

3 Your Voice and AI Conversations

🍎 Apple Intelligence (on-device) Your voice and all AI processing happen entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. No conversation ever leaves your phone.
🤖 Claude by Anthropic (cloud) Your spoken words are transcribed by Apple's on-device Speech Recognition framework, then sent directly from your device to Anthropic's API using your own API key. We never see, store, or process your conversations. The connection goes: your phone → Anthropic. iTrucker is not in that path.

Anthropic's privacy policy governs how they handle API requests: anthropic.com/privacy

4 Location Data

The app uses your location for:

  • Finding nearby fuel stations, rest areas, and truck stops
  • Detecting state-line crossings for automatic IFTA mileage logging
  • Displaying your current position on the route map

Location is processed on your device. We do not transmit your GPS coordinates to our servers — we don't have any. If iCloud sync is enabled, your trip mileage records (which include state codes but not precise GPS coordinates) sync through your own iCloud account.

The app requests While Using the App location permission only. It does not track your location in the background.

5 HealthKit

HealthKit integration is entirely opt-in. If you enable it:

  • We read heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), and sleep data to calculate a fatigue score
  • That fatigue score is displayed to you in the Health tab
  • If you have a dispatcher, they see only a binary Fatigue Alert: Yes / No — never your raw health numbers
  • Your raw HealthKit data never leaves your device under any circumstances

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

6 iCloud and CloudKit Sync

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, certain records sync between your devices and with your dispatcher through Apple's CloudKit:

  • Dispatch messages
  • Driver profile (name, duty status, speed, fuel level — no health data)
  • Trip records and IFTA mileage
  • Maintenance reports

This sync uses your own iCloud account and is subject to Apple's iCloud privacy policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

If iCloud is not configured, all data remains local to your device only.

7 Your Claude API Key

Your Anthropic API key is stored in the iOS Keychain — the same secure, encrypted storage used for passwords and financial credentials on iOS. It is never:

  • Sent to our servers
  • Included in crash reports or analytics
  • Accessible to other apps on your device

You can remove your API key at any time from Settings → AI Configuration → Remove.

10 Your Rights and Choices

Delete your data

Because your data lives on your device and in your own iCloud account, you control it entirely. Delete the app to remove all local data. Delete iCloud records in Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Revoke permissions

You can revoke location, microphone, speech recognition, and HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

California residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request deletion — contact us at the address below.

EEA / UK residents (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, and port your personal data. Since your data lives on your own device and iCloud account, you exercise these rights directly through iOS. For any questions or requests, contact us at clarkdjcr@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

12 Security

We follow Apple's recommended practices for on-device data security:

  • API keys stored in the iOS Keychain (AES-256 encrypted)
  • All network requests use HTTPS / TLS
  • iCloud sync is end-to-end encrypted for sensitive data per Apple's policies
  • No data is stored on third-party servers we control
